Threat Scope & Adversary Goals:

Attackers leverage multi-channel social engineering (SMS spoofing / Smishing, Spear-phishing, fake KYC updates, utility bill cut-off threats) to harvest banking credentials, OTPs, Aadhaar/PAN identity numbers, or advance clearing fees.
